What to notice

In a slow squeeze, the system recovers from small knocks more and more slowly as it nears the threshold, so its readings drift further from their average and the variance climbs. That’s the early warning. In a sudden shift, the system never gets weaker: conditions simply change faster than it can follow, and the variance gives no warning until the collapse is already under way. The statistic can also be early, late or wrong in a single noisy run, so run each type several times.
