Small models you can break

Four interactive simulations of things that are hard to grasp from text: how networks fall apart, why some searches are fast, how languages split, and whether a collapse can be seen coming. Each page is self-contained. Open it, change something, watch what happens.

What these are, and aren’t

  • They are deliberately simple models meant to build intuition. They are not predictions about any real lake, reef, language or network.
  • The statuses above say only what has actually been checked. “Not yet tested in a browser” means exactly that.
  • Next on the list: a “Sources and assumptions” section on each page, using published papers, and browser tests that run automatically.
